ABSTRACT Street‐level bureaucrats (SLBs) directly affect citizens, making them central to public service delivery. Service success therefore hinges on public sector organizations' ability to recruit competent SLBs. We argue that citizens' perspectives should inform SLB recruitment, especially for contact‐intensive, relational frontline roles. Yet public administration research has not integrated citizens' perspectives into SLB recruitment, despite a broader shift toward a service‐user orientation.
