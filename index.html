Expansive Public Service Delivery: Advancing Public Value Governance

ABSTRACT Public administration has traditionally emphasized the normative ideal that public agencies have a responsibility to improve the lives of communities through public service delivery. We turn to public libraries to explore possibilities for what we call Expansive Public Service Delivery. Expansive Public Service Delivery is defined by a mission to serve all, professional and ethical responsibilities, and proactive responsiveness through outreach and (re)assessment. Public libraries have long aimed to improve communities and advance democracy by providing open, free information access to all. Today, they also proactively seek to expand their services to underserved groups ensuring their relevance and mission achievement. Democratic Backsliding and Governance Outcomes: Measuring Administrative and Political Channels of Decline

ABSTRACT Democratic backsliding has emerged as a defining political challenge of the contemporary era, yet systematic cross‐national evidence linking it to governance outcomes remains scarce.
